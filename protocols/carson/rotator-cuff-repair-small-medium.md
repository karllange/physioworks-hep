# Rotator Cuff Repair - Small to Medium Tear

## Phase 1: Passive Motion (Weeks 0-4)

Precautions: No active shoulder abduction or external rotation past 30 deg.

### Wand Shoulder Flexion
- Sets: 2
- Reps: 20
- Hold: 5 sec
- Frequency: 3x/day
- Cues: Use the uninvolved arm to lift the wand only to comfort. Keep the elbow soft. Do not hike the shoulder.

### Standing Pendulum
- Sets: 2
- Reps: 20
- Hold: 2 sec
- Frequency: Daily (7x/wk)
- Cues: Lean forward and support the body with the uninvolved arm. Let the involved arm hang and make small circles. Keep the shoulder relaxed.

### Scapular Retraction and Depression
- Sets: 2
- Reps: 10
- Hold: 5 sec
- Frequency: Daily (7x/wk)
- Cues: Sit tall and gently draw the shoulder blades down and together. Do not shrug. Stay below the pain limit.

## Phase 2: Active Assisted Motion (Weeks 4-8)

Precautions: No resisted lifting. External rotation only to the limit set by the surgeon.

### Wand Shoulder Flexion
- Sets: 2
- Reps: 15
- Hold: 5 sec
- Frequency: Daily (7x/wk)
- Cues: Use the uninvolved arm to lift the wand only to comfort. Keep the elbow soft. Do not hike the shoulder.

### Supine Passive External Rotation
- Sets: 2
- Reps: 10
- Hold: 5 sec
- Frequency: Daily (7x/wk)
- Cues: Lie on your back and hold a wand with both hands. Let the uninvolved arm roll the involved forearm outward. Stop at the first stretch or the surgeon's limit.
