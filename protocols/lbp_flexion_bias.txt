Protocol: Lumbar Flexion Bias (Spinal Stenosis / Facet Arthropathy)
Phase: Acute to Subacute
Category: Spine
Type: standard
Precautions: Avoid end-range lumbar extension, prolonged standing, and overhead reaching with arched back. Siting or forward leaning should relieve symptoms.
Exercises:
- Single Knee-to-Chest: 2 sets, 3 reps, 30s hold, 2-3x daily. Supine, draw one knee gently toward chest until gentle stretch is felt in lower back.
- Double Knee-to-Chest: 2 sets, 3 reps, 30s hold, 2-3x daily. Supine, bring both knees toward chest, holding behind knees to open lumbar foramen.
- Posterior Pelvic Tilt: 2 sets, 12 reps, 5s hold, 2x daily. Supine hooklying, flatten lower back into table by contracting abdominals and rotating pelvis backward.
- Seated Lumbar Flexion: 2 sets, 8 reps, 5s hold, 2x daily. Sit in chair, slowly bend forward sliding hands down shins toward ankles within comfortable tolerance.
- Cat-Camel: 2 sets, 10 reps, 3s hold, 2x daily. Quadruped, alternate gently between rounding spine toward ceiling and relaxing to neutral.
