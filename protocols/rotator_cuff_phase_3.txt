Protocol: Rotator Cuff Repair (Phase 3)
Phase: Weeks 8-12
Precautions: Avoid impingement positions under heavy resistance. Gradual progression of load; pain must remain below 3/10 during and after exercise.
Exercises:
- Band External Rotation at 0° with Towel Roll: 3 sets, 10 reps, 2s hold, 1x daily. Light resistance, elbow pinned to side with towel roll.
- Band Internal Rotation at 0°: 3 sets, 10 reps, 2s hold, 1x daily. Elbow at 90 degrees at side, rotate inward across stomach.
- Standing Scaption (Full Can): 3 sets, 10 reps, 2s hold, 1x daily. Raise arms in scapular plane (30 deg forward of coronal plane), thumbs up, stop at 90 deg.
- Prone Horizontal Abduction (T's): 3 sets, 10 reps, 3s hold, 1x daily. Prone, arms straight out to side at 90 deg, lift thumbs toward ceiling.
- Scapular Retraction and Depression: 3 sets, 12 reps, 5s hold, 1x daily. Squeeze blades back and down.
