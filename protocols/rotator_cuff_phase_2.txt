Protocol: Rotator Cuff Repair (Phase 2)
Phase: Weeks 4-8
Precautions: Wean from sling per MD. No sudden lifting > 2 lbs. Avoid active abduction above 90 degrees until scapular control is restored. No aggressive stretching into end-range external rotation.
Exercises:
- Wand Shoulder Flexion: 2 sets, 10 reps, 5s hold, 1-2x daily. Active-assisted flexion to tolerance (target 120-140 deg).
- Side-Lying External Rotation: 2 sets, 10 reps, 2s hold, 1x daily. Keep towel roll under arm, lift forearm without rotating torso.
- Prone Scapular Retraction: 2 sets, 12 reps, 3s hold, 1x daily. Prone on table, squeeze shoulder blades together without shrugging toward ears.
- Serratus Wall Slide: 2 sets, 10 reps, 3s hold, 1x daily. Forearms on wall, slide upward maintaining contact and upright posture.
- Sleeper Stretch: 2 sets, 3 reps, 30s hold, 1x daily. Gentle posterior capsule stretch into internal rotation within comfortable tolerance.
