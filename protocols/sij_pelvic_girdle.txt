Protocol: SI Joint & Pelvic Girdle Stabilization
Phase: Subacute / Functional
Category: Spine
Type: standard
Precautions: Avoid wide-stance asymmetric lunging or aggressive unilateral shear forces during acute pain. Keep pelvis level and square during all movements.
Exercises:
- Isometric Hip Adductor Squeeze with Ball: 3 sets, 10 reps, 5s hold, 2x daily. Supine hooklying, squeeze small ball or yoga block between knees to activate adductors and stabilize pubic symphysis.
- Clamshell: 3 sets, 12 reps, 2s hold, 1x daily. Side-lying, knees bent 90 degrees, rotate top knee upward keeping heels glued together and pelvis strictly forward.
- Side-Lying Hip Abduction: 3 sets, 10 reps, 2s hold, 1x daily. Top leg straight, slightly extended and internally rotated, lift toward ceiling without rolling hips.
- Supine Glute Bridge with Loop Band: 3 sets, 10 reps, 3s hold, 1x daily. Mini-band around knees, push outward into band resistance while bridging.
