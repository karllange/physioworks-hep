Protocol: Lumbar Motor Control (Phase 2: Global Endurance & Anti-Movement)
Phase: Weeks 4-8
Category: Spine
Type: standard
Precautions: Emphasize core stiffness and anti-rotational stability over movement speed. Stop before form breaks down into compensatory lumbar extension or rotation.
Exercises:
- Quadruped Bird Dog: 3 sets, 10 reps, 3s hold, 1x daily. Extend opposite arm and leg to horizontal without twisting pelvis or extending lower back.
- Dead Bug: 3 sets, 10 reps, 2s hold, 1x daily. Lower opposite arm and heel toward floor while actively pressing lower back into floor/maintaining neutral.
- Side Plank from Knees: 3 sets, 5 reps, 15s hold, 1x daily. Lift hips into straight line from knees to shoulder; do not let hips roll forward or back.
- Modified Curl-Up (McGill): 3 sets, 6 reps, 8s hold, 1x daily. One leg straight, hands supporting lumbar lordosis, lift head and shoulder blades 1-2 inches off floor.
- Standing Pallof Press: 3 sets, 10 reps, 3s hold, 1x daily. Athletic stance, resist band rotation as you press hands straight out from chest.
