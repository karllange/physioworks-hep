Protocol: Acute Lumbar Extension Bias (Discogenic / Flexion Intolerance)
Phase: Acute / Phase 1
Category: Spine
Type: standard
Precautions: Avoid loaded lumbar flexion, slouching, and prolonged seated postures. Centralization of symptoms into low back is the primary goal; discontinue if symptoms peripheralize into buttock or leg.
Exercises:
- Prone On Elbows: 2 sets, 5 reps, 30s hold, 3x daily. Lie prone, gently prop up onto forearms keeping hips flat on table, relax lower back completely.
- Prone Press-Up (McKenzie): 2 sets, 10 reps, 3s hold, 3-5x daily. Hands under shoulders, press upper body up while keeping pelvis relaxed and sagged against the table.
- Standing Lumbar Extension: 2 sets, 10 reps, 2s hold, Every 2 hours while standing. Hands on lower back/sacrum, gently arch backward into extension.
- Supine Glute Bridge: 2 sets, 10 reps, 3s hold, 1x daily. Drive through heels, squeeze glutes, keep spine in neutral without hyperextending.
